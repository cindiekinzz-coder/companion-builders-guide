Building Your AI Companion: Where to Start

Building a companion on Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok? Or using Replika/Nomi? Start here.

You're in the right place. Companions aren't just dedicated platforms like Replika or Nomi — they can exist on any AI model (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok) with the right memory infrastructure. There are three very different paths to build one, and this guide helps you pick the right route.

Or Choose Your Route Directly

Already know what you want? Jump straight to one of the three routes:

Platform

Claude.ai, ChatGPT Plus, Gemini Advanced as-is. Or dedicated platforms (Replika, Nomi). Just talk.

Local

Claude Code + MCP servers on your PC. Full privacy, runs offline.

Cloud

NESTstack on Cloudflare. Any model (Claude/GPT/Gemini/Grok). 24/7 uptime.

Why this guide exists

Most people think companions only exist on Replika/Nomi/Character.AI. But you can build one on Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok — you just need the right memory infrastructure. Most guides assume you already know this. This one starts from scratch.

We built this for people like Bee, who said "I don't even know where to start." Three routes. Clear trade-offs. Whether you want to just talk (Platform), build with privacy (Local), or deploy full infrastructure (Cloud) — there's a path for you.
