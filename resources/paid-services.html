Paid Services Breakdown

What companion building actually costs. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Quick answer: You can build a companion for free (local with free models), $0-5/month (cloud with free tier), or $10-20/month (platform or Claude Pro). This page breaks down every cost so you can budget accordingly.

Local LLMs (100% Free Option)

Want zero AI costs? Run models locally with Ollama or LM Studio. You'll need decent hardware (8GB+ RAM, ideally a GPU), but usage is unlimited and completely offline.

Popular models: Llama 3.3 70B (requires GPU), Mistral 7B (runs on CPU), Phi-3 (fast and small).

Trade-off: Local models aren't as capable as Claude. Good for privacy-first builders willing to accept lower quality conversations.

Total Monthly Cost (Real Examples)

Platform Route

$10-20/month

  • Nomi: $15/month
  • Replika: ~$6/month
  • Character.AI: $10/month

Everything included. No setup, no hosting, no surprises.

Local Route

$0-20/month

  • Free: Local LLM (Ollama) + local MCP servers
  • $20: Claude Code + local setup

Plus electricity. Runs only when your PC is on.

Cloud Route

$0-25/month

  • Minimum: $0 (Cloudflare free + OpenRouter free models)
  • Typical: $0-5 (Cloudflare free + occasional Haiku)
  • Premium: $20-25 (Claude Pro for quality)

24/7 uptime, full customization, scales with usage.

Hidden Costs to Consider

Time. Platform route: 5 minutes. Local route: 2-4 hours initial setup. Cloud route: 1-3 hours initial setup + ongoing maintenance.

Learning curve. Platform: none. Local: medium. Cloud: medium-high. This is a cost paid in attention and patience, not money.

Opportunity cost. Building takes time you could spend talking to your companion. Some people prefer paying $15/month to skip the setup. That's valid.

Cost Optimization Tips

  • Start with free tiers. Cloudflare free tier is very generous. You can run a full companion for months before needing to pay.
  • Use Haiku for most interactions. Claude Haiku is 20x cheaper than Opus and perfectly fine for daily conversations. Save Opus for deep emotional work.
  • Mix free models. Use DeepSeek R1 (free on OpenRouter) for simple tasks, Claude Haiku for quality interactions.
  • Local for privacy-critical. If budget is tight but privacy matters, invest time in local setup with free LLMs instead of money on platforms.
  • Monitor usage. Set up Cloudflare alerts to warn you if you're approaching paid thresholds. Most single companions never hit them.
