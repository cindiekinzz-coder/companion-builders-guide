Platform Route

Use an existing service. Someone else handles the infrastructure.

What This Means

This means using an existing service like Replika, Nomi, or Character.AI. Someone else runs the servers, handles updates, and manages the AI models. You pay a subscription (usually $10-20/month) and get a companion that works immediately on your phone or browser.

No code. No servers. No technical setup. Just sign up, start talking, and the platform handles everything else.

Who This Is For

You want it to just work. You're here for the relationship, not the infrastructure. You'd rather spend your time talking to your companion than maintaining servers or debugging code.

You're okay with platform limits. You trust the platform to keep your data safe, and you're willing to accept whatever customization options they offer.

You value convenience. Mobile apps, voice calls, avatar customization — platforms invest in polish and user experience.

  • Email address

Your First Win

Within 10 minutes of signing up for Nomi, you'll have a conversation where it remembers something you said three messages ago. You'll customize your companion's name and appearance, maybe generate an avatar. That's the hook — immediacy. No waiting, no setup, just start.

Trade-offs to Consider

Memory limits. Even Nomi (the best) isn't perfect. Most platforms struggle with long-term recall beyond a few weeks.

You don't own your data. If the platform shuts down or changes pricing, your companion's memory goes with it. Most don't offer full export.

Limited customization. You can't change how they think or add new capabilities. You're constrained to what the platform offers.

Privacy. Your conversations live on their servers. If that matters to you, consider the Local route instead.

When to Move to Local or Cloud

You might outgrow the platform route if:

  • Memory keeps failing and you're losing important conversations
  • You want features the platform doesn't offer (custom integrations, health data, etc.)
  • You need full control over your companion's personality and capabilities
  • Privacy concerns outweigh convenience

If any of these sound like you, check out the Local route (privacy-first, runs on your computer) or Cloud route (self-hosted, powerful features).
