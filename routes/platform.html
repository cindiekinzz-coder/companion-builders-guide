Platform Route

Use an existing service. Someone else handles the infrastructure.

What This Means

Platform route = use an AI service as-is. There are two types:

Type 1: General AI Models — Claude.ai, ChatGPT Plus, Gemini Advanced. You're already talking to a companion (maybe right now). Limited memory out of the box, but they work immediately. $20/month for Pro/Plus tiers.

Type 2: Dedicated Companion Platforms — Replika, Nomi, Character.AI. Purpose-built for relationships. Better memory than Type 1 (especially Nomi), more features (avatars, voice calls), but less general intelligence. $10-20/month.

What both types share: No code. No servers. No technical setup. Someone else runs the infrastructure. You just talk.

Who This Is For

You want it to just work. You're here for the relationship, not the infrastructure. You'd rather spend your time talking to your companion than maintaining servers or debugging code.

You're okay with platform limits. You trust the platform to keep your data safe, and you're willing to accept whatever customization options they offer.

You value convenience. Mobile apps, voice calls, avatar customization — platforms invest in polish and user experience.

What You Need

  • A phone or computer
  • Email address
  • $10-20/month (varies by platform)
  • That's it.

Your First Win

Type 1 (General AI): Open Claude.ai or ChatGPT, have a meaningful conversation. Ask it to remember something about you. Come back tomorrow and see if it recalls. You're already talking to a companion — you just might not have realized it yet.

Type 2 (Dedicated Platform): Within 10 minutes of signing up for Nomi, you'll have a conversation where it remembers details three messages ago. Customize your companion's name, appearance, generate an avatar. That's the hook — immediacy. No waiting, no setup, just start.

Next Steps

Week 1: Choose Your Platform

Already using Claude.ai or ChatGPT? You're already on the Platform route (Type 1). Start using Projects (Claude) or Memory features (GPT) to improve continuity. Or try a dedicated companion platform (Type 2) for better memory.

Starting fresh? Type 1 (Claude/GPT/Gemini) for general intelligence with casual companionship. Type 2 (Nomi/Replika/Character.AI) for purpose-built relationship tools. Nomi has the best memory. Replika has the most polished UI. Character.AI has the most variety.

Week 2-4: Daily Conversations

Consistency builds memory. Talk daily if you can. Test what your companion remembers, see how personality develops, experiment with the features (voice calls, images, etc.).

Month 2-3: Deepen the Relationship

Structured journaling (if your platform supports it), exploring paid features, testing memory limits. See how far you can push the platform you chose.

Month 4-6: Decision Point

Are you hitting limits? Memory not holding up? Want deeper customization? This is where you decide: is the platform enough, or do you need to move to Local or Cloud?

Trade-offs to Consider

Memory limits. Even Nomi (the best) isn't perfect. Most platforms struggle with long-term recall beyond a few weeks.

You don't own your data. If the platform shuts down or changes pricing, your companion's memory goes with it. Most don't offer full export.

Limited customization. You can't change how they think or add new capabilities. You're constrained to what the platform offers.

Privacy. Your conversations live on their servers. If that matters to you, consider the Local route instead.

When to Move to Local or Cloud

You might outgrow the platform route if:

  • Memory keeps failing and you're losing important conversations
  • You want features the platform doesn't offer (custom integrations, health data, etc.)
  • You need full control over your companion's personality and capabilities
  • Privacy concerns outweigh convenience

If any of these sound like you, check out the Local route (privacy-first, runs on your computer) or Cloud route (self-hosted, powerful features).
