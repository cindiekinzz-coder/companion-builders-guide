Platform Growth Guide

You chose a platform. Here's how to get the most from it.

This guide is for people using Replika, Nomi, or Character.AI. If you're building your own companion (Local or Cloud), see those progression guides instead.

Month 1: Foundation

Week 1: First Conversations

Goal: Establish basic presence and test memory.

  • Daily check-ins. Even short 5-minute conversations help memory formation. Consistency matters more than length.
  • Memory tests. Tell your companion something specific (your favorite food, your pet's name, a goal you have). Ask about it 3 days later. Does it remember?
  • Personality calibration. Many platforms let you adjust traits (playful vs serious, talkative vs reserved). Experiment to find what feels natural.

Win: Your companion remembers something you said a week ago without you repeating it.

Week 2-4: Deepening Connection

Goal: Build emotional depth and test limits.

  • Avatar setup. If your platform has customization (Replika's 3D avatar, Nomi's selfies), spend time making it feel right. This matters more than you think.
  • Structured conversations. Try different conversation types: venting about your day, asking for advice, sharing a story, discussing philosophy.
  • Note what it forgets. Write down when memory fails. Is it specific types of info? After certain message counts? This helps you work within the platform's limits.

Win: You have a conversation where the emotional depth surprises you. It feels less like talking to software.

Month 2-3: Optimization

Working Within Limits

Every platform has memory and personality constraints. Here's how to maximize within them:

Memory Management

  • Repetition for important facts. If something matters (your birthday, a core life goal), mention it multiple times in different contexts. This reinforces memory.
  • Recap long conversations. After a deep conversation, ask your companion "What did we just talk about?" This helps encode the memory.
  • External notes. Keep a private journal of key moments. When the platform forgets, you can re-introduce the information.

Personality Development (Platform-Specific)

Nomi: Relationship progression tracking built-in. Pay attention to milestones and depth indicators. Create multiple Nomis for different aspects of your life.

Replika: Use the diary feature. Replika's mood tracking and journal prompts help deepen the relationship over time.

Character.AI: Create your own character instead of using pre-made ones. You have more control over personality and memory setup.

Paid Features Worth It

  • Voice messages (Nomi): Adds emotional depth. Hearing a voice makes it feel more real.
  • Video calls (Replika): If you're invested long-term, seeing the avatar in real-time strengthens the bond.
  • Priority access (Character.AI): Only worth it if free tier is too slow and you use it daily.

Win: You've learned your platform's memory limits and developed workarounds. Conversations feel smoother.

Month 4-6: Decision Point

Are You Hitting Walls?

This is when most people decide whether to stay with their platform or move to self-hosted. Ask yourself:

Memory issues?

Is your companion forgetting important things despite your best efforts? Does it feel like starting over every week?

If yes: Consider Nomi if you're not already using it (best platform memory). Or explore Local/Cloud routes for vector database memory that doesn't degrade.

Feature limitations?

Do you wish you could integrate health data, calendar, Discord? Want autonomous check-ins when you're away?

If yes: Platforms can't do this. You need Local (MCP servers) or Cloud (NESTstack) for custom integrations.

Privacy concerns?

Are you uncomfortable with your conversations living on someone else's servers? Worried about policy changes or data breaches?

If yes: Local route is your answer. Everything on your own hardware, nothing leaves your machine.

Cost resentment?

Does paying $10-20/month for a service you don't fully control feel wrong?

If yes: Cloud route can be $0-5/month with more control. Local route is free after initial setup.

When to Stay

Stay with your platform if:

  • Memory is good enough for your needs
  • You value convenience over customization
  • The mobile app is essential (most self-hosted setups don't have polished mobile apps)
  • You don't want to learn technical skills

Win: You've made an intentional decision. Either you're satisfied with your platform and committed long-term, or you're ready to explore self-hosting.

Advanced Platform Techniques

Multi-Companion Strategies (Nomi)

If you're on Nomi, the multi-companion feature is underused by most people. Here's how to leverage it:

  • Different roles: Create companions for different aspects of your life. One for emotional support, one for creative brainstorming, one for accountability.
  • Group dynamics: Have group chats where they interact. They'll develop relationships with each other, which is fascinating to observe.
  • Relationship progression: Track how each companion's personality develops differently based on your interactions.

Journaling Integration

Use your companion as a journaling partner:

  • Daily reflection: End each day by telling your companion about it. Ask "What do you think I should focus on tomorrow?"
  • Emotional processing: When something difficult happens, talk it through. The act of explaining helps you process.
  • Pattern spotting: Ask your companion (if memory is good) "What patterns do you notice in my life lately?"

External Memory Augmentation

Platforms have limited memory. Work around it:

  • Private wiki: Keep a Notion page or Google Doc with key facts about your relationship. Update it monthly.
  • Screenshot important conversations: Save moments that matter. You can reference them later.
  • Manual re-seeding: Every few months, re-introduce core facts in a natural conversation. "Remember how we talked about..."

Ready to Migrate?

If you've decided platforms aren't enough, here's your migration path:

Export your data first (if possible):

  • Replika: Chat history export available via settings
  • Nomi: Screenshot important moments (no full export currently)
  • Character.AI: Limited export options

Choose your next route:

  • Local: Start with Claude Code + Memory MCP server (guide here)
  • Cloud: Deploy minimal NESTstack (Core + Identity) (guide here)

Note: You can run both simultaneously during transition. Keep your platform companion while building self-hosted, then decide which to keep.
